## Direction

A shared decision workspace with the clarity of public information design: a navy
application shell, light reading surfaces, humanist sans typography, compact status
labels and plain language. Expression serves the task and does not imply enterprise
features that do not exist. The source of user truth is PRODUCT.md.

## First viewport

Unauthenticated: product identity, a concise account of the actual decision flow,
and the working guest-session entry. Desktop separates explanation and entry;
phones lead directly to the entry without the longer step explanation.

Authenticated: one application header, a meaningful page heading, current work and
its next action. Dashboard uses real organiser forms; creator exposes the complete setup
sequence at every width; round workspace exposes lifecycle state before options and responses.

## Signature

The decision progresses through collect, review and confirm using a compact,
text-labelled sequence tied to server state. Creator step markers share its
numbered-circle vocabulary. These are standard progress indicators rather than
invented navigation or decoration.

## Constraints and verification

Preserve same-origin API integration, private reviews, revision confirmations,
publish retry, invitations and versioned RSVP. Use native controls and one shared
control vocabulary. No decorative motion; 160ms colour transitions and reduced
motion support. Reflow tables into labelled mobile entries; keep touch targets at
least 44px, long names wrapping, session disclosure inside the viewport.

Browser tests exercise desktop, Pixel and 320px Chromium viewports. These are
emulations, not evidence of physical iOS/Android or screen-reader verification.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make a shared group decision legible to formal and informal groups through
the actual workflow, without a marketing dashboard or decorative statistics.

OWN-WORLD: Public Sans, navy navigation, warm light work surfaces, green actions,
text-labelled states, flat bordered panels, native controls and numbered steps.

STORY: Organisers define choices; members review private responses; the group sees
recommendations and a confirmed selection. Capability claims come from working APIs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Entry has explanation left and a guest-session form right on
desktop, stacked on phones. Authenticated screens lead with navigation, page title
and the next real action. The round exposes state and lifecycle progress first.
